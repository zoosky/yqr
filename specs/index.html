yqr Specs

The living issue tracker and design record for yqr. Every feature, bug, architecture decision, implementation convention, and research note lives here as a markdown spec with a **Status:** field.

  • features/ – feature specs (yqr-fNNN-*), plus the status tracker
  • bugs/ – bug reports and their resolutions (yqr-bNNN-*)
  • architecture/ – cross-cutting design documents (yqr-aNNN-*)
  • implementation/ – system conventions and ops specs (yqr-mNNN-*)
  • research/ – evaluations and investigations (yqr-rNNN-*)
  • marketing/ – positioning and content plans (yqr-kNNN-*)

In this section

  • Feature Specs

    Feature specifications (yqr-fNNN-*). Each spec carries a **Status:** field; yqr-f0000-feature-status.md tracks every feature across epics.

  • Bug Specs

    Bug reports and resolutions (yqr-bNNN-*). yqr-b000-bug-status.md tracks every bug and its current state.

  • Architecture Specs

    Cross-cutting design documents (yqr-aNNN-*), such as the fidelity surgical-edit architecture.

  • Implementation Specs

    System specifications, fact sheets, and non-functional requirements (yqr-mNNN-*). These documents are the source of truth for cross-cutting concerns such as the CI/release process and the shared test corpus.

  • Research Notes

    Evaluations and investigations (yqr-rNNN-*), such as the jq feature-gap analysis and the noyalib fidelity evaluation.

  • Marketing

    Positioning, content, and go-to-market notes (yqr-kNNN-*). Everything here is about how yqr is explained to people who have not used it, as opposed to how it is built.
